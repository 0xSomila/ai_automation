# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Practice owners, reception staff and practitioners checking appointments during the working day. Catalyst 7 also uses the fictional Meridian practice to demonstrate the WhatsApp receptionist to prospective clients.

## Product Purpose

Connect the patient's WhatsApp conversation to a clear practice schedule and practitioner updates. The dashboard makes bookings and changes visible alongside conversations that need attention.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, capable. The user approved the green and warm-white practice dashboard concept in output/imagegen/practice-dashboard-concept.png.

## Design Principles

- Make the daily schedule the primary surface.
- Show which appointment changed and retain its reference.
- Keep demonstration data explicitly labelled and separate from pilot records.
- Use plain language and familiar controls.
- Show the actual capability and state of each connection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, keyboard focus, labelled inputs, readable contrast and reduced-motion support as implementation defaults.
