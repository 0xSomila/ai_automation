Meridian WhatsApp Booking Privacy Notice

Catalyst 7 (Pty) Ltd is responsible for the personal information it handles in this WhatsApp booking service. Our business address is ParlourPalm Street, Karanpark, Pretoria, South Africa.

Who this notice covers

This notice applies to anyone who contacts Meridian through its WhatsApp booking service or whose booking information someone shares through it. Share another person’s information only if they have authorised you to do so.

The current pilot uses a fictional practice. During testing, use a fictional name and appointment details. Keep symptoms, diagnoses, medical records, identity documents and payment information out of the chat.

What we collect and why

When you message the service, WhatsApp supplies your phone number, message text and a message identifier. We store the conversation and automated replies. If you make an appointment, we also store its service, date, time, status, booking reference and calendar event ID. The calendar event includes the name and service you give the assistant.

We use this information to reply to enquiries, check availability, manage bookings, prevent duplicate processing and troubleshoot the service. Using WhatsApp is voluntary. The assistant needs your WhatsApp number and message to reply, and a name and appointment details to make a booking. You can contact Catalyst 7 by email to ask about the service.

Who handles the information

  • Meta and WhatsApp carry messages between your account and the service.
  • Cloudflare delivers incoming messages to the booking server.
  • Anthropic’s Claude processes the conversation text and booking context to generate replies.
  • Supabase stores conversation and booking records. The pilot database is hosted in Ireland.
  • Google Calendar stores bookings and checks availability.

These providers may process information outside South Africa, where data protection laws can differ. Catalyst 7 must assess overseas transfers and the protections that apply to them under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Retention and deletion

Catalyst 7 keeps the Meridian WhatsApp booking records it controls for 30 days from collection and then queues them for deletion, unless applicable law requires longer retention. Database cleanup runs every 15 minutes. Calendar cleanup runs while the pilot computer is online and signed in. Cancelling a test appointment leaves the conversation and a cancelled booking record in the pilot database until those records are deleted.

To request earlier deletion where POPIA permits, email Somila at catalyst7@catalyst7.co.za. Include the WhatsApp number you used or your booking reference so we can locate the records.

Your rights and how to contact us

You can ask to access or correct your personal information, object to processing, or request deletion where POPIA permits. You can also ask a person to review a booking or reply generated by the assistant. Somila handles privacy requests for Catalyst 7 at catalyst7@catalyst7.co.za. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

You can lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa by emailing POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za or using its complaints page.
